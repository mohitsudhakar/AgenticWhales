How it works

Three steps, no predictions anywhere

1 · Show it your trades

Screenshot, CSV, PDF statement, or a read-only brokerage connection. Guests are analyzed in memory and never stored.

2 · Get the dollar number

Deterministic arithmetic on your own fills quantifies each behavioral leak — what it cost, past tense — plus the process rule that addresses it.

3 · Watch it shrink

Sign in and every audit is saved: your discipline score trends, flagged patterns get re-tested on your newer trades, and the pre-trade check guards your next entry.

Options traders
